# How to play Conquer

Be the first to reach **10 victory points** (or whatever the table agreed on) by building villages, cities and
roads across an island of resource-producing land.

## The board

The island is made of hex tiles. Each tile produces one of five resources: **Wood**, **Brick**, **Sheep**,
**Wheat** or **Stone**. The **Wasteland** produces nothing and is where the raider starts.

Each producing tile carries a number from 2 to 12. The dots under the number show how likely it is to be rolled:
the more dots, the more often it pays out. Red numbers (6 and 8) are the most common rolls and are never placed
next to each other.

**Harbors** sit on the coast. Build a village or city on a harbor's two corners to trade at its better rate.

## Setting up

Players place pieces in a snake order: first player to last, then last player back to first. Each time it's your
turn you place **one village and one road** touching it, for two of each in total. Your second village immediately
collects one of each resource from the tiles around it. Villages can't be built on a corner next to another
village or city, anywhere, at any time.

## Your turn

1. **Roll the dice.** Every tile showing the total pays: each village on its corners gets 1 of its resource, each
   city gets 2. If the bank runs short of a resource, nobody gets it, unless only one player is owed it, in
   which case they get whatever is left.
2. **Trade.** With the bank at 4:1, at a harbor at 3:1 or 2:1, or offer a trade to the other players.
3. **Build and play cards**, in any order, as long as you can pay.
4. **End your turn.**

### Rolling a 7: the raider

Nothing produces on a 7. Instead:

- Anyone holding **more than 7 cards** discards half of them, rounded down.
- The roller moves the **raider** to another tile and steals one random card from a player with a building on it.

While the raider sits on a tile, that tile produces nothing.

## Building

| Build | Cost | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Road** | 1 Wood, 1 Brick | Extends your network. New villages must connect to your roads. |
| **Village** | 1 Wood, 1 Brick, 1 Sheep, 1 Wheat | 1 victory point, and 1 resource per matching roll. |
| **City** | 2 Wheat, 3 Stone | Upgrades one of your villages. 2 victory points, and 2 resources per matching roll. |
| **Action card** | 1 Sheep, 1 Wheat, 1 Stone | A random card from the action deck. |

Each player has 15 roads, 5 villages and 4 cities.

## Action cards

You may play **one** action card per turn, and not on the turn you bought it. Soldiers can be played before you
roll.

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Soldier** | Move the raider and steal a card, just as if you'd rolled a 7. |
| **Engineers** | Build 2 roads for free. |
| **Harvest** | Take any 2 resources from the bank. |
| **Plunder** | Name a resource. Every other player hands you all of theirs. |
| **Victory point** | Worth 1 point. Kept hidden until it wins you the game. |

## Awards

- **Great Road** (2 points): the first player with an unbroken road of at least 5 segments. Anyone who builds a
  longer one takes it over. An opponent's village can cut a road in two.
- **Grand Army** (2 points): the first player to play 3 Soldiers. Anyone who plays more takes it over.

## Winning

The game ends the moment a player reaches the target score **on their own turn**, counting their hidden victory
point cards.

## House rules

Press **House rules** at any point to change the game for everyone. The game log announces every change.

| Rule | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Points to win | 10 |
| Discard when holding more than | 7 cards |
| Bank trade ratio | 4:1 |
| Harbor ratios | 3:1 and 2:1 |
| Great Road minimum | 5 roads |
| Grand Army minimum | 3 soldiers |
| Friendly raider (can't target players with 2 or fewer points) | Off |
| Re-roll 7s during the first rounds | Off |
| One action card per turn | On |
| Play an action card the turn you buy it | Off |
| Trade during anyone's turn | Off |
| Extra starting resources | None |

### Wild cards

Before the first roll you can also shuffle wild cards into the action deck. They take effect the moment they're
bought, and everyone sees which one it was.

| Card | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Golden Crown** | Whoever draws it wins on the spot. |
| **Curse** | Whoever draws it is out of the game. |
| **Windfall** | Take 3 random resources from the bank. |
| **Tribute** | Every other player gives you 1 random card. |
| **Plague** | Every other player loses half their cards. |
| **Bankruptcy** | Lose every resource card in your hand. |
| **Earthquake** | One of your roads is destroyed. |
| **Bandits** | Move the raider and steal, right away. |

## Bigger boards

On the new-game screen you can grow the island from the standard 19 tiles up to 127. The bank, the action deck and
the harbors grow with it, so a big board doesn't run dry.

## Tips

- Spread your starting villages over different resources and numbers; a village on 6, 8 and 5 beats one on 2, 12
  and 11.
- Stone and Wheat build cities, and cities double your income. Don't neglect them.
- Watch the leader. A well-placed raider can slow down whoever is closest to winning.
